## About DevKit

DevKit is a curated knowledge base, not an application or a substitute for a tool's own documentation. It is designed for students, beginners, and experienced developers who want a useful starting point without installing anything.

## How to use

1. Choose a category from the table above or browse the directory indexes.
2. Open the page for your task and compare the listed options.
3. Check the pricing note and limitations before relying on a service.
4. Follow the official website or documentation link for current details.
5. [Suggest a resource](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/resource-suggestion.md) or [report a broken link](.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/broken-link.md).

No programming knowledge or account is needed to read this repository. Some third-party services may require an account.

## Project principles

- Prefer free and accessible resources, and label free tiers accurately.
- Prefer official documentation and primary sources.
- Explain usefulness in beginner-friendly language.
- Favor practical, reputable recommendations over exhaustive lists.
- Recheck links and pricing when making changes; service terms can change.
- Welcome community contributions and corrections.

**Pricing note:** Labels describe the resource's general access model, not a guarantee. Plans, free quotas, and terms change; check the provider's current pricing page before use.
