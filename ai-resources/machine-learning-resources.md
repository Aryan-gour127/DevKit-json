| Resource | Useful for | Audience and pricing |

| [scikit-learn user guide](https://scikit-learn.org/stable/user_guide.html) | Classical ML estimators, preprocessing, and evaluation | Python learners; free and open source |
| [PyTorch tutorials](https://pytorch.org/tutorials/) | Tensors, neural networks, and training workflows | Deep-learning learners; free and open source |
| [TensorFlow tutorials](https://www.tensorflow.org/tutorials) | TensorFlow and Keras examples | Deep-learning learners; free and open source |
| [Hugging Face course](https://huggingface.co/learn) | Transformers and open-model workflows | Learners; free course content, hosted compute may cost |
| [Kaggle datasets](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets) | Finding datasets for learning and experiments | Learners; platform terms and dataset licenses vary |
| [Papers with Code](https://paperswithcode.com/) | Research papers, benchmarks, and implementations | Researchers and practitioners; access is generally free |

## Evaluation fundamentals

Use a held-out test set, compare against a simple baseline, select metrics appropriate to the task, and inspect subgroup performance. Avoid data leakage. For datasets and pretrained models, read licenses, provenance, intended use, and known limitations before redistribution or deployment.
