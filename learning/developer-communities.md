# Developer communities

Use community spaces respectfully: search before posting, share a minimal reproducible example, describe what you tried, and remove secrets and personal data.

| Community | Useful for | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Stack Overflow](https://stackoverflow.com/) | Focused technical questions and searchable answers | Follow its question guidance and tagging rules |
| [GitHub Discussions](https://docs.github.com/en/discussions) | Questions and conversations around a project | Use the relevant repository's community rules |
| [DEV Community](https://dev.to/) | Articles and peer discussion | Verify technical advice and protect personal information |
| [freeCodeCamp Forum](https://forum.freecodecamp.org/) | Learning and beginner programming questions | Include context and what you have tried |
| [Python community](https://www.python.org/community/) | Python events, groups, and community links | Find official community channels |
| [MDN Community](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/community) | Contributing to and discussing web documentation | Review contribution guidance |

Community membership is generally free to access, but local events or third-party services may have separate costs.
