# Interview preparation

Use free resources first and check current platform plans. Paid subscriptions may unlock additional practice, mock interviews, or course material.

| Topic | Resource | Pricing/access |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Programming fundamentals | [CS50x](https://cs50.harvard.edu/x/) | Free course access; certificate options may be paid |
| Data structures and algorithms | [Open Data Structures](https://opendatastructures.org/) | Free textbook |
| DSA practice | [LeetCode](https://leetcode.com/) | Freemium; premium content is paid |
| Object-oriented programming | [Refactoring.Guru](https://refactoring.guru/design-patterns) | Articles are freely accessible; books/courses may be paid |
| Databases and DBMS | [CMU Database Systems course materials](https://15445.courses.cs.cmu.edu/) | Course materials available free; terms may vary by offering |
| Operating systems | [Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~remzi/OSTEP/) | Free online book |
| Computer networks | [Cloudflare Learning Center](https://www.cloudflare.com/learning/) | Free educational articles |
| Web development | [MDN Learn](https://developer.mozilla.org/en-US/docs/Learn) | Free |
| AI and ML fundamentals | [Google ML Crash Course](https://developers.google.com/machine-learning/crash-course) | Free |

Prepare by explaining tradeoffs aloud, writing tests, clarifying requirements, and reviewing mistakes. Avoid memorizing answers without understanding.
